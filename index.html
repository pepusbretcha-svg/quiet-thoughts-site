The Noticing

Short reflections, one a day.

The Noticing is a small YouTube Shorts channel. Each video is a single idea — about time, memory, attention, regret, change — read over quiet imagery, in under a minute.

You can watch it at youtube.com/@thenotiicing.

About this page

This site exists because the channel is published by a small program that uploads through the official YouTube Data API, and Google asks every application that uses its API to have a homepage and a privacy policy. That is all it is here for.

The program is a personal tool. It is not offered to anyone else, it has no users other than its author, and it asks Google for exactly one permission: to upload videos to its own channel.
